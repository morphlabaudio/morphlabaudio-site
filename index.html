Installing

The installer puts the plugin in the right folder. Then rescan plugins in your DAW.

macOS

Open the .pkg and follow the steps. VST3 and AU are installed together. Logic users: the plugin appears under Audio Units → Morph Lab Audio.

Windows

Run the .exe. The VST3 goes to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3. If SmartScreen appears, choose “More info” → “Run anyway”.
