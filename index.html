03 — InstallmacOS · Windows

Installing

The installer puts each format in the right folder. Afterwards, rescan plugins in your DAW.

macOS

Open the .dmg and double-click the .pkg inside. AU and VST3 are installed together.

If macOS says it can’t verify the developer: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click “Open Anyway”. You only need to do this once per plugin.

Windows

Unzip the download and run Setup.exe. The VST3 goes to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.

If SmartScreen appears, click “More info” → “Run anyway”.

Not showing up?

Rescan plugins in your DAW. In Logic, the AU is listed under Audio Units → MorphLab.

Still nothing? Send a bug report with your OS and DAW and I’ll help.
